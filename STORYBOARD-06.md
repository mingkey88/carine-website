# Storyboard: design 06, "Album"

Design 06 (`previews/06-album.html`) keeps the watercolour look and the scroll-told build of design 05 and changes
what the page is about. Design 05 shows what Carine does. Design 06 is about the visitor's life: the site is **an
album that is not finished yet**, and Carine is the person who helps plan for the pages still to come.

Built and pushed to the staging link on 23 Nov 2026. It is a preview: not production, and kept out of search like
the rest of the site. Design 05 is
unchanged.

---

## The idea

A financial planner's real subject is the future, and a memory is the future looked back on. Three moves carry it:

1. **The future tense.** The headline is "Some of your best memories haven't happened yet." Her own line,
   "Financial planning for every stage of life.", sits directly beneath it.
2. **Her four areas move to the backs of the photographs.** The front of each print is a moment; the back is the
   planning behind it, in her approved words. Nothing she wrote was removed or reworded.
3. **The visitor leaves with a first page of their own.** They tap the pages they are working towards and send
   them to Carine on WhatsApp, so her line "good financial planning starts with a conversation, not a sales pitch"
   becomes what the page does.

What carries the eye from first screen to last: cream album pages, photo corners on every print, pencilled
captions in her handwriting face, a page number in the header, and one day's light (paper, golden hour, night).

## The interactions

1. **Turn a print over** (section 4). With motion on, each print is a button: a tap, Enter or Space turns it. The
   scroll turns them in order; a print the visitor has turned by hand stays the way they left it. With reduced
   motion or no JavaScript nothing is hidden: each print shows its picture with her words beneath.
2. **Start your album** (section 11). Eight choices: A first home, Getting married, Starting a family, Children's
   education, Seeing the world, Caring for parents, Retiring well, Something else. Each ticked choice is pasted
   onto "your first page" as a small print. "Send my first page to Carine" opens `wa.me/6583893356` with, for
   example, "Hi Carine, I'd like to talk about planning for: a first home, retiring well." followed by the
   visitor's note if they wrote one. Choices, not free text, so nobody has to type private details. Nothing is
   stored. Without JavaScript the choices are hidden and the note alone goes to WhatsApp, as in design 05.
3. **The lens, the rain and the video row** work as in design 05.

None is needed to reach the end of the page.

## Compliance notes

- **Illustrations, not client stories.** No client photos, names, stories or testimonials. The only people shown
  are Carine (her own likeness, which she has agreed to) and figures seen from behind. The footer says so.
- **Plan for, never promise.** New wording stays with "working towards" and "planning for". Nothing says a
  memory, a home or a retirement will happen. "Some of your best memories haven't happened yet" is about the
  future in general; compliance should confirm they read it that way.
- **Gentle protection.** Weather only. No illness, accident or loss is shown.
- **No figures, products or returns.** The plant is a plant: nothing is on it but leaves.
- **The blueprint and the model are pictures of planning, not of a product or a result.** They are the same
  cottage scene as the painting, drawn first as a plan and then as a white card model. No letters, numbers,
  prices, floor areas or labels are on them (checked on the originals). Nothing says a visitor will own a home;
  compliance should confirm they read "plan, model, picture" as a figure of speech for planning.
- **The album's five prints** are objects, not people or events that happened: no client's memory is shown.
- **Her descriptions appear twice** (on the back of each print and in each topic section). Same words both times.
- **The page chooser** sends nothing anywhere itself; it only opens WhatsApp with text the visitor can read and
  edit before sending. "Retiring well" and the other choices are the visitor's own aims, not outcomes on offer.
- Everything in design 05's compliance notes (`STORYBOARD.md`) still applies to the parts carried over.

## Two habits Carine could adopt (her decision, not built)

"Building memories with the client" is only honest if it also happens in her practice. Two ideas for her; the page
says nothing about either unless she takes them up:

- **The yearly page.** She opens each annual review by asking what that year's big moment was, and closes by
  asking which one they are planning for next.
- **A keepsake at milestones.** When a client reaches one (the keys, a birth, retirement), she sends a small
  watercolour of that moment in the site's style. Her firm's gifts policy needs checking first.

## Art

Two pictures were made for this design on 23 Nov 2026, in the same ChatGPT chat as the rest (prompts in
`previews/art/ART-BRIEF-05.md`): the cottage painting redrawn as a **blueprint** (`11-blueprint`) and as a **white
card model standing on that blueprint** (`12-model`). ChatGPT drew both over the original painting, so the three
line up to the pixel and one can turn into the next. Everything else is from `previews/kit/wc2/`. The album itself
(cover, pages, mounts) is drawn in CSS: no picture file.

No video was made. Both sections were built in code instead of with a Flow clip, for three reasons: a clip cannot
keep the five approved pictures and their captions exact; a scroll can stop on any frame, and a frame half-way
through an AI morph is a smear while a frame half-way through these is still a clean picture; and it costs no
credits and about 290 KB instead of a megabyte or more. A Flow clip (Omni 1.2 Flash) of the blueprint lifting into
the model is a possible later step if the cross-over between the two states should feel more physical.

With a third art pass the album could show real scenes instead of single objects: keys in a front door, a school
bag by the door, a packed suitcase, a long table at dusk, two cups on a garden table.

## How it is built

`06-album.html` is one self-contained file made from `05-story.html`: the same engine (tall sections with a sticky
stage, one progress value per section, motion added only when a script runs and reduced motion is off, the still
page as the default). New parts: photo corners (`.cornered`, one pseudo-element), the prints that turn (`rotateY`,
`aria-pressed`), the page chooser, and:

- **The album (section 2).** Real CSS 3D, no library and no picture. `.album` sets the perspective; `.book` is
  the camera; `.book__base` is the back board with three sheets stacked a hair apart (the page edge you see when
  it tilts); `.book__leaf` is the cover, hinged on the spine, with two faces: navy cloth outside, and inside the
  card that becomes the left-hand page. The scroll sets `--o` (cover open) and `--c` (camera round) on the book,
  and `--b`/`--e` on each print (how far it has come down, plain and eased). On screens up to 900 px there is one
  page instead of a spread, and the cover swings away off the left edge. With reduced motion or no JavaScript the
  album is simply open, flat, with every print in place.
- **Plan, model, picture (section 3).** Three images in one frame. `--c` stands the sheet up, `--m` wipes the
  model in from the ground up, and the design-05 lens shows the painting and then opens over all of it. The lens
  only follows the pointer once the sheet is upright. With reduced motion or no JavaScript only the painting is
  shown, and the plan and model are not downloaded.
