Who I work with

I specialise in empowering young working adults in Singapore through expert financial planning. My mission is to guide them to create a robust financial strategy, ensuring they can retire early with confidence.

What I talk about

Four themes I keep returning to, with clients and in what I write.

Motherhood & Money

What changes financially when you become a parent, and the decisions that are far easier to make early than late.

Family Protection

Insurance, income protection, critical illness and debt protection. What each one is actually for, and when it is not needed.

Building Wealth

Investing, wealth accumulation and long term planning, explained without the jargon that usually surrounds it.

Preparing Our Children

Money habits, financial literacy, and what AI and a changing world of work mean for the generation coming after us.

How working together looks

No obligation at the first conversation, and nothing recommended before I understand what you are actually planning for.

We talk

A first conversation about where you are now, who depends on you, and what you want to be true in ten years. No products, no forms.

I map it out

Where your protection gaps are, what your money is currently doing, and what your options cost. In plain language, in writing.

We review it, regularly

Plans age. We revisit yours as your income, your family and your priorities change, rather than filing it away and hoping.
